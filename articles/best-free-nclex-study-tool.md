## Detailed Reviews

### 1. ClarityNCLEX — Best Overall Value
ClarityNCLEX offers over 4,000 premium questions with full NGN question types, including case studies and standalone questions aligned with the Clinical Judgment Measurement Model (CJMM). Their readiness exams simulate the actual test experience, and detailed rationales help you learn from mistakes. At just $9.99/month, it provides exceptional value for students seeking NGN readiness without the premium price tag of legacy providers. The platform includes performance tracking and personalized study plans.

### 2. UWorld — The Established Standard
UWorld remains the gold standard for nursing question banks, renowned for its high-quality, challenging questions and detailed explanations. Their NCLEX Q bank features over 2,000 questions with NGN-style items added in recent updates. While the interface is traditional and lacks some modern NGN innovations, the question quality is unmatched. At $109/month, it's a significant investment best suited for students prioritizing question quality over budget and who prefer a traditional question bank format.

### 3. Archer Review — Budget-Friendly Traditional
Archer Review offers a more affordable traditional Q bank with over 1,500 questions and solid rationales. Their content covers core nursing concepts thoroughly, though NGN alignment is limited compared to newer platforms. At $49/month, it's a budget-friendly option for students who prefer classic multiple-choice questions and want to supplement their studying with affordable practice. However, those seeking comprehensive NGN preparation may need additional resources.

### 4. Kaplan — Comprehensive Course Option
Kaplan provides a full NCLEX prep course including live classes, on-demand lessons, and a question bank with over 1,000 questions. Their NGN readiness has improved in recent updates, and they offer structured study plans. However, at $500+ per month, it's the most expensive option on this list. Kaplan suits students who want an all-inclusive course with instructor support and are willing to pay for a structured, guided prep experience.

### 5. Bootcamp — Video Learner Focus
Nursing Bootcamp focuses on video-based content with engaging lectures and mnemonic aids. Their question bank varies in size but includes NGN-style questions. While the video format appeals to auditory and visual learners, the question bank depth may not match dedicated Q banks. Pricing starts around $200+, making it a mid-range option for those who prioritize video instruction over extensive practice questions.

### 6. Free Tools — Supplemental Only
Free resources like NurseAchieve, Nursing.com's free trial content, and Open Nurse Project offer limited practice questions and content reviews. While useful for supplementing paid resources or reviewing specific topics, they lack the comprehensive NGN-aligned question banks and readiness exams needed for thorough preparation. Best used as additional practice alongside a primary study tool.
