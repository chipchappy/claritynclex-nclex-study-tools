## What Is a 30-Day NCLEX Study Plan?

A 30-day NCLEX study plan is a fixed-length schedule that splits the NCLEX test plan into daily
blocks so you always know what to review, how many practice questions to answer, and when to take a
readiness assessment. Thirty days is long enough to cover all eight client-needs categories at least
once, and short enough to keep retention high before test day.

## Free NCLEX Study Plan (Day-by-Day Structure)

| Week | Focus | Daily questions | Deliverable |
|------|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| Week 1 | Fundamentals, Safe & Effective Care Environment | 50 | Baseline readiness score |
| Week 2 | Pharmacology, Physiological Adaptation | 75 | Drug-class cheat sheet |
| Week 3 | Med-Surg, Reduction of Risk Potential, NGN case studies | 100 | 2 CJMM case studies/day |
| Week 4 | Maternity, Peds, Psych + full-length simulation | 100 | 2 timed readiness exams |

Each planner page includes a question-count tracker, a rationale-review checkbox, and a weak-topic
log so you can see which client-needs category is dragging your score down.

## How to Use the Free Planner

1. Print all 30 pages (or use it as a fillable PDF on a tablet).
2. Take a baseline readiness exam on Day 1 and write the score at the top of Week 1.
3. Answer the daily question target — **always read every rationale**, including for questions you
   got right.
4. Log every missed question's topic in the weak-topic column.
5. Re-test at the end of Week 2 and Week 4. A 10-15 point jump between assessments is the signal
   you're on pace.
