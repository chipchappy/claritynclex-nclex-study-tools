# Free NCLEX Study Material and Websites (2026)

Free **NCLEX study material** is everywhere — the hard part is using it without getting scattered. This list covers the most useful free NCLEX websites and how to fit them into a real plan.

## Best free NCLEX websites by job

- **RegisteredNurseRN** — fundamentals, procedures, disease processes, labs. Best for content refresh.
- **Nurselabs** — topic quizzes and care-plan examples. Best for weak-area drills.
- **SimpleNursing (free)** — mnemonics and visual explanations. Best for memory hooks.
- **Official NCSBN** — exam format, NGN structure, scoring. Best for orientation.
- **YouTube nursing educators** — second explanations of confusing topics.
- **Quizlet** — flashcards for labs, meds, isolation. Verify accuracy (user-generated).

## Free material already paid for through school

Before buying anything, check what tuition covers:

- **ATI** and **HESI** access
- **Elsevier** products and ebooks
- Library databases and faculty remediation modules
- Textbook companion question banks

## The gap free websites don't fill

Free sites are strong for content but weak for **daily mixed practice, NGN case studies, and readiness checks**. The NCLEX is application-based, so you need a practice layer, not just review material.

## Closing the gap affordably
