# NCLEX Practice Questions: Where to Find Free and Affordable Qbanks (2026)

**Practice questions are the single most important part of NCLEX prep.** The exam is computer-adaptive and measures clinical judgment, so the bulk of your study time should go to answering questions and reviewing rationales — not just re-reading notes.

## What makes a good NCLEX practice question?

- **NGN item types** — case studies, bow-tie, matrix, highlight, drag-and-drop, select-all-that-apply (SATA).
- **Rationales that explain the distractors** — you should understand why the wrong answers are less safe, not just why the right one is correct.
- **Mixed topics** — the NCLEX jumps between systems, so your sets should too.
- **Readiness feedback** — a CAT-style exam that estimates your pass probability.

## Free NCLEX practice questions

Free sources are good for daily warm-ups and targeted gaps:

- **Nurselabs** — topic-level quizzes (fundamentals, meds, systems).
- **Nurse Plus Academy** — free and paid drills.
- **YouTube educators** — walkthroughs of individual questions.
- **Official NCSBN** — sample items straight from the exam source.

Limitation: free sets rarely match the full complexity of NGN case studies and are not a reliable readiness signal on their own.

## How to use practice questions effectively

1. Do 25–50 mixed questions daily.
2. Review every rationale — right and wrong.
3. Log misses in an error log (topic, missed cue, safety issue, next action).
4. Take a readiness exam weekly.
5. Let your miss patterns decide what to study next.
